Ukubukeka okusha: imibala empunga, izithombe zangemuva zezindawo zokusebenza nezithonjana ezintsha. Amakhasi okwamukela asetha i-Dejavu ekuqaleni. Ibha yekheli ingaba phezulu noma phansi. Swayipha phezulu ibha yezenzo uthole imenyu entantayo, manje ene-“Ithebhu entsha”, “Londoloza ikhasi” ne-“Sula idatha”. Amathebhu asondela futhi ahlehla esikrinini sasekhaya, ahlala ephapheme uma ushintsha izinhlelo. Kanye nezilungiselelo ngamaqembu, ukulayisha okusheshayo ne-Firefox 159.
